Why Self-Hosted?

Choosing to self-host Supabase on a VPS wasn't just about reducing costs—it was about control.

  • Total data ownership and privacy.
  • Bypassed expensive managed-BaaS scaling limits.
  • Provided hands-on experience with Docker, server hardening, and DevOps workflows.

Under the Hood: Hardening

Production readiness goes beyond functional code.

  • Security: Strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies in Postgres ensuring tenant isolation.
  • Infrastructure: Hardened VPS with firewall rules and secure SSH practices.
  • Reliability: Automated database backups and edge function monitoring.
